LipScan 0.5.0 (voice → lip-sync and face curves)

Version 0.5.0 on Oct 26 (MIT, runs locally): tracks a real face on video (52 blendshapes, head, gaze) to replay it on a rig, or turns a voice alone into mouth, blink and brow curves, exported as ARKit 52 for Blender, Unity, Unreal.

Stylized 2D Mouth Rig (2D mouth for Blender)

Blender 5.2 add-on launched Oct 20 at $5: one click puts a stylized 2D mouth on any head, driven in Pose Mode from a floating panel, with 28 expression and lip-sync presets, 4 styles, customizable teeth and tongue.
