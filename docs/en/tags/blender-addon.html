RBF Nodes 4

Paid add-on ($17.99 on Superhive) by Brave Rabbit: a node-based RBF solver to drive several values from several inputs (shoulder correctives, facial rigs). v4 rebuilds the solver, handles rotations better and adds a free runtime. v3 rigs must be rebuilt.
