reanimate-motion (video → BVH on any character)

Published Nov 7 (MIT): this command-line kit tracks the body frame by frame in a video, cleans and solves it into a BVH with Mixamo bone names, then keys your Blender rig or a three.js character. Runs locally, with checks proving each rule.

RuiC-2D (one illustration → rigged, lip-synced 2D avatar)

Published Nov 6 (MIT): from a single front-facing illustration, this local editor splits the parts, adds a deformable mesh, syncs the mouth to your voice, animates blinks and tail on a timeline and runs from your webcam (MediaPipe) into OBS.

face-builder (photos → rigged GLB avatar, no generative AI)

Updated Nov 5 (Apache-2.0): 100% local pipeline (Blender + MPFB) that builds a MakeHuman body, projects your photo, dresses from CC0 and retargets walk/idle/type/sit into an animated .glb. No generative AI; author quotes ~6/10 likeness.

November 3, 2026

JustRigIt (auto-rig + local video mocap)

Published Nov 3: film a move on your phone, it captures it locally with NVIDIA’s GEM-X (fingers included), plants the feet, rigs your character if needed and exports for Unreal, Unity, Godot or Blender. AGPL-3.0; capture on Windows with an 8 GB+ RTX GPU.
