video2live2d (video → Live2D motion)

Command-line tool (MIT, Linux and macOS) released Oct 17 that turns a face-and-shoulders video into a Live2D Cubism motion3.json for VBridger models: head, eyes, brows, mouth, breathing, auto calibration. No iPhone, no live tracking.

October 17, 2026

anny-maya 1.2.1 (Anny parametric human in Maya)

Maya 2022–2027 module (Apache-2.0, v1.2.1 of Oct 16) bringing NAVER’s Anny human model (CC0 data): age and body shape sliders, 52 ARKit shapes, optional Google GNM head, then bake to a classic Maya rig (joints, skinCluster, blendShapes) ready for animation and FBX.

October 15, 2026

Character DNA Addon 0.14.4 (MetaHuman → Blender)

GPL-3.0 Blender add-on (free base, paid Pro version) that imports MetaHuman DNA files with face board and correctives via OpenRigLogic, then sends them back to Unreal. 0.14.x (Oct 9–14) switches RigLogic to Z-up and fixes eyes/mouth. Blender 4.5/5.2.

October 12, 2026

Auto Face Mocap Pro

New paid Blender add-on (from $99 personal, $129 commercial, GPL) released October 11: it applies facial capture from a simple webcam or phone video to a character with ARKit shape keys. Blender 5.2. Launch offer −30%.
