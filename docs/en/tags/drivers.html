ParaRig 1.2.0

Free Blender add-on (GPL-3.0) that generates a panel of sliders to drag in the viewport and wires the drivers to shape keys, bones or properties by itself, with no expressions to write. 1.2.0 (Oct 14) drives X/Y/Z of the same bone from a single slider.

Driver Espresso Lite 1.6.6

Free Blender add-on (GPL-3.0): 18 ready-to-use driver “recipes” (oscillation, blinking, looping rotation, fades, ease/spring, RGB neons) with a live curve preview and offset across several objects or bones. Native drivers: the animation survives uninstalling.
