Stylized 2D Mouth Rig (2D mouth for Blender)

Blender 5.2 add-on launched Oct 20 at $5: one click puts a stylized 2D mouth on any head, driven in Pose Mode from a floating panel, with 28 expression and lip-sync presets, 4 styles, customizable teeth and tongue.

October 20, 2026

Lightweight CPU VTuber Tracker 0.1.0 (GPU-free webcam VRM mocap)

Native Windows webcam tracker in Rust (v0.1.0, Oct 19) that drives a VRM avatar on the CPU alone: 52 ARKit blendshapes, gaze, arms, torso and fingers at 30 fps. Code is Apache-2.0/MIT, but the bundled body model is under the SAM License: read it before pro use.

VisemeMaker 1.0.0 (VRChat visemes from A-I-U-E-O)

Unity tool (MIT) released Oct 16 that builds VRChat’s 14 visemes from the 5 vowel shapes most models already have (MMD, VRoid, Source…) and wires lip sync on the avatar descriptor, with preview and strength control.

October 18, 2026

video2live2d (video → Live2D motion)

Command-line tool (MIT, Linux and macOS) released Oct 17 that turns a face-and-shoulders video into a Live2D Cubism motion3.json for VBridger models: head, eyes, brows, mouth, breathing, auto calibration. No iPhone, no live tracking.

anny-maya 1.2.1 (Anny parametric human in Maya)

Maya 2022–2027 module (Apache-2.0, v1.2.1 of Oct 16) bringing NAVER’s Anny human model (CC0 data): age and body shape sliders, 52 ARKit shapes, optional Google GNM head, then bake to a classic Maya rig (joints, skinCluster, blendShapes) ready for animation and FBX.

Character DNA Addon 0.14.4 (MetaHuman → Blender)

GPL-3.0 Blender add-on (free base, paid Pro version) that imports MetaHuman DNA files with face board and correctives via OpenRigLogic, then sends them back to Unreal. 0.14.x (Oct 9–14) switches RigLogic to Z-up and fixes eyes/mouth. Blender 4.5/5.2.
