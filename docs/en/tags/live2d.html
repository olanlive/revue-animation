Tsukumoe (open alternative to Live2D Cubism)

Repo started Oct 30, Apache-2.0 or MIT: a 2D rigging and animation platform aiming to load Live2D (.moc3) and Inochi2D models, with a CLI, a wgpu renderer and planned lip-sync. Very early stage, not usable as an app yet.

Live2D Offline Converter 0.3.0 (Live2D → Blender bones)

Experimental Blender add-on (MIT, v0.3.0 on Oct 19) that samples a Live2D clip and converts it into a native Blender armature, weights and Action that play without the plugin. 0.3 bakes masks and blend modes (Cycles). You supply the Cubism SDK yourself.

L2DEditor (Live2D-style 2D puppet rigging)

Open-source (Apache-2.0) 2D puppet editor, a Cubism alternative published Oct 19: PSD import, meshing and auto-rig, keyforms, physics, .moc3 export without Live2D binaries, plus a CLI and an MCP server so AI agents can drive it.

October 18, 2026

video2live2d (video → Live2D motion)

Command-line tool (MIT, Linux and macOS) released Oct 17 that turns a face-and-shoulders video into a Live2D Cubism motion3.json for VBridger models: head, eyes, brows, mouth, breathing, auto calibration. No iPhone, no live tracking.
