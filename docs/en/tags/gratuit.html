Free Cascadeur plugin (v1.0 on Oct 19, Windows): import MMD .vmd motions onto your characters, export VMD as FK or IK and clean up the generated rig. Heads-up: no license, binary only, the repo holds just the docs.

XR Animator 0.36.0 (AI webcam mocap)

Full-body webcam mocap (MediaPipe) for VRM/MMD avatars, web or desktop: 0.36.0 (Oct 17) adds a faster “lite” body model, a GPU/CPU choice and local web app mode on Android and iOS. Free, but the repo declares no license.

CEB SAM 3D Body Loader (AI mocap in Blender, now free)

Since Oct 17, Carlos Barreto’s add-on is free: video mocap (GEM-X, SAM 3D Body) in Blender 4.0 to 5.2, retargeting to Auto-Rig Pro, foot-sliding fix, walk along a curve. Windows only; some underlying models are restricted to non-commercial use.

Riginsec

New free Blender add-on (Gumroad, pay what you want) released October 5: it rigs a surface and generates its weights in seconds so you can animate it right away. A very young project, little documented and with no stated licence: test it on a working copy.

Found via: Blender Artists — Riginsec announcement
