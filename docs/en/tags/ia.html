anymove (mocap for any creature from an AI video)

Published Oct 27 under Apache-2.0: an AI video shows the creature front and side in one frame, a DINOv2 reader finds every joint and the rig (wings, tails, eight legs) is fitted frame by frame, then exported as GLB. Needs an NVIDIA GPU and the paid fal API.

prompt2rig (prompt → Mixamo character animation, fingers included)

Type a motion, a video model acts it out, then GVHMR + WiLoR (added Oct 20) extract body and fingers for a Mixamo character, .glb export to Blender or Unity. Apache-2.0 code, but GVHMR, SMPL-X and WiLoR are non-commercial and video goes through fal (paid).

Local web app (MIT, published Oct 20): give it a rigged character, it trains a DINOv2 detector on renders of your rig, then fits the skeleton to a two-view AI video. Outputs a Blender Action, .blend and FBX; tested on quadrupeds.

ComfyUI-GEM-X (NVIDIA GEM-X video mocap → FBX)
