CTRL_DEF 0.1.2 (auto controllers from DEF bones)

Released Oct 24 (GPL-3.0, Blender 4.2 to 5.2): select your DEF_ bones and the add-on generates colored FK controllers with shared custom shapes in one click, then binds them without dependency cycles. Same author as Auto Copy Transforms DEF (seen Oct 23).
