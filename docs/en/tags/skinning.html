Auto Dangle Rig 1.9.0 (bone chains for skirts, hair, ribbons)

Paid Blender 5.2 add-on (¥300, BOOTH): builds dangle bone chains from a selection, one per skirt column, mirrors ribbons, weights by transfer or distance, then previews the jiggle on a Mixamo/VRMA clip. Announced Oct 22.

October 20, 2026

Riggle 0.1.1 (rig + jiggle bones, Blender-powered)

Local rigging workbench (MIT, heavy WIP) driven by Blender: fit a 22-joint humanoid skeleton to your mesh, mark what should wobble (jiggle bones), then export rigged GLB + FBX with a spring manifest. Portable Windows build on Oct 19.

October 13, 2026

MeshRig 0.2.0

New free standalone app (GPL-3.0, Windows/macOS/Linux) that rigs a mesh from a .blend: humanoid, quadruped or chain skeleton fitted inside the volume, weights computed through the volume, weight brush, auto IK and pose test. Drives Blender (tested on 5.2) in the background.
