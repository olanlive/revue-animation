anny-maya 1.2.1 (Anny parametric human in Maya)

Maya 2022–2027 module (Apache-2.0, v1.2.1 of Oct 16) bringing NAVER’s Anny human model (CC0 data): age and body shape sliders, 52 ARKit shapes, optional Google GNM head, then bake to a classic Maya rig (joints, skinCluster, blendShapes) ready for animation and FBX.

RigFlow (animation transfer for Maya)

Maya tool (MIT, online Oct 16) that transfers body animation between characters with different proportions and naming: automatic joint matching with a confidence score, manual tweaks, HumanIK preview before baking, face untouched. Alpha, Maya 2023–2024, no release.

mGear 5.4.0

Free Maya rigging framework (MIT): 5.4 (Oct 14) adds Facial Rigger 3.0 (unified eyes/lips/brows), Anim Picker 2.0 (SVG, widgets), Blocking Ghosts (3D onion skinning), Spring Manager 2.0 and ~2× faster Shifter builds. Maya 2022–2027.
