video2live2d (video → Live2D motion)

Command-line tool (MIT, Linux and macOS) released Oct 17 that turns a face-and-shoulders video into a Live2D Cubism motion3.json for VBridger models: head, eyes, brows, mouth, breathing, auto calibration. No iPhone, no live tracking.

CAST (video mocap for any skeleton)

MIT code + CAST Live Blender add-on released Oct 16: captures motion from an RGB video and transfers it in real time to skeletons with a different topology (crab, quadruped…). MIT weights; DINOv3 backbone (Meta licence) and SAM2 to be supplied separately, CUDA GPU required.

Found via: X — post by @Evan_THU (co-author), Oct 16

ComfyUI-GEM-X (NVIDIA GEM-X video mocap → FBX)

ComfyUI nodes (Apache-2.0, Oct 15): full-body mocap (77 joints, hands and face) from a single video with NVIDIA GEM-X, then retarget onto Mixamo, Unreal or Unity FBX with a built-in 3D preview. Weights under the NVIDIA Open Model License, ~10 GB of models, NVIDIA GPU required.

Kinesia

Multi-person 3D mocap from a single video (CC0 code): SAM 3.1 + SAM 3D Body + DINOv3, tracking and re-identification, feet anchored to the ground, web viewer. Heavy (Linux, ~32 GB NVIDIA GPU), Meta models under a restrictive SAM licence, CSV/JSON export (no BVH/FBX announced).
